Pill is the brand's only button: a 48px, 50px-radius, bold pill. Use `variant="blue"` on light grounds, `"white"` or `"ghost"` on navy/royal, `"ink"` on light accent bands (coral, lime, peri).

```jsx
<Pill variant="blue" href="/fit-check">Take the free AI Fit Check</Pill>
<Pill variant="ghost" href="/services">See solutions</Pill>
```

Props: `variant` (blue|white|ghost|ink), `small` (36px nav CTA), `href` or `onClick`.
