TextLink renders the brand's bold tertiary link (`.tert`) or arrow link (`.arrow`, appends →). Use `tone="dark"` on navy grounds (white + underline for contrast), `tone="ink"` on light accent bands.

```jsx
<TextLink kind="arrow" href="/services">See all services</TextLink>
<TextLink tone="dark" href="/fit-check">Start with the free Fit Check</TextLink>
```
