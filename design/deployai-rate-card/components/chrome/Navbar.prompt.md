Navbar is the absolute-positioned overlay header used over navy heroes: white logo + bold white links with a lime hover underline, plus a ghost pill CTA. Set `logoBase` to the relative path of `assets/` from your page.

```jsx
<Navbar logoBase="../../assets" />
```

Props: `links`, `cta`, `variant` ("dark" overlay / "light"), `ctaVariant`. Collapses to a burger menu under 900px.
