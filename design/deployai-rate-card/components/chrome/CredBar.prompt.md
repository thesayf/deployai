CredBar is the white credential band under heroes: an uppercase label over a row of grayscale badge images that colourize on hover. Badge art lives in `assets/badges/`.

```jsx
<CredBar label="Anthropic Partner Network · Claude Certified" badges={[
  { alt: "Anthropic Partner", src: "../../assets/badges/partner-logo.png" },
  { alt: "Claude Certified Architect", src: "../../assets/badges/architect-pro-logo.png" },
]} />
```
