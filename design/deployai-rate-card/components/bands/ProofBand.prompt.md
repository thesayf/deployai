ProofBand is the P09 stat band: prose column left, big 48px weight-300 numerals right (lime on the dark statnavy ground, navy on lavender). Stats carry attributed descriptions.

```jsx
<ProofBand heading={<>Almost nobody's <em>winning</em> with it.</>} prose={["The problem was never the model."]}
  statsIntro="The gap, in numbers"
  stats={[{ n: "95%", d: "of AI pilots deliver no measurable return. (MIT, 2025)" }]} ground="statnavy" />
```
