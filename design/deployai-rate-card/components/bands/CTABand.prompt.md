CTABand is the M17 final CTA — the ONE coral band per page. Black text, `pill p-ink` / `tert ink` CTAs, white calendar card on the right.

```jsx
<CTABand heading={<>Let's find your <em>first</em> deployment.</>}
  paragraphs={["A 30-minute call with a senior consultant."]}
  fineNote="No obligation. A senior consultant, not a sales rep."
  ctas={[{ label: "Book a call", href: "#book", className: "pill p-ink" }]} />
```
