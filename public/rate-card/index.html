Training

One day, at your office or at Wemeet in Muscat, where we hold our regular AI trainings, working on your own files.

Every person picks the job they repeat most often. By the end of the day that job is running with AI, they have done it themselves twice, and they have written down what it used to cost in time and what it costs now.

What you get
  • A saved setup that does their own repeated task, ready to use again tomorrow
  • A one-page before-and-after, in their own numbers: minutes saved per task, hours saved per year
  • A team list of every repetitive job in the room, with the three biggest at the top
  • A 45-minute follow-up call, booked before they leave, for whatever breaks in the first fortnight
Your time

One day per person, plus a 30-minute brief from you beforehand and a 45-minute follow-up call two weeks later.

The day
Morning
What these tools actually do, shown live on real work. When to use a chat and when to use an agent, which most teams get wrong. How to set the tools up so they already know your company before anyone types anything.
Afternoon
Each person names the job they repeat most. Each person builds it. Each person runs it twice on real work, with a facilitator beside them.
Last half hour
The team ranks the jobs that need more than a day to fix. That list is yours to keep.

How we work

Fixed price, fixed scope. The fee is agreed in writing before we start. We do not bill by the hour and we do not run open-ended discovery.

You decide at every step. Every stage ends with a decision that is yours. You can stop after the audit.

The people who scope it build it. The same faces from the first call through to a running system.

Your data stays where you put it. Your information is stored and processed in the region you choose, in your own account. Nothing you send is used to train the model. Access is limited and time-limited. We put all of this in writing before we build anything, in a form you can hand to your compliance team.
